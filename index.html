What I sell

I build and sell software: plugins for JetBrains IDEs, apps for Windows on the Microsoft Store, WordPress plugins, and data tools on the Apify Store. Each app does one job well, opens with sensible defaults and needs no setup. Support comes straight from the developer: send a quick email for help, bug reports or feature requests.

Products are sold as one-time purchases or as monthly and yearly subscriptions. Some have a free version and a paid plan with more features.
